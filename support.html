Answers to common questions, plus a way to reach the developer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start the free trial?

Open Quick Canvas and click Start 7-Day Free Trial on the welcome screen. Nothing is charged, now or when the trial ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

Quick Canvas becomes view only: pictures still open and zoom, but drawing, editing and saving ask you to unlock for US$4.99 once.

I bought Quick Canvas on another Mac. How do I get it here?

Open the welcome screen, or click Unlock in the status bar, and choose Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does reinstalling restart the trial?

No. The trial and the purchase are tied to your Apple ID by the App Store.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

The fill leaks out of my shape.

The outline has a small gap. Zoom in with the magnifier, close the gap with the pencil, and fill again.

The arrow keys do not move my selection.

Click once on the canvas so it has keyboard focus, then press the arrow keys. Hold Shift to move 10 pixels at a time.

The AI image button says it is not available.

AI image creation needs a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15.2 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. Remove Background needs macOS 14 or later.

Can I save with a transparent background?

Not currently. Quick Canvas saves solid pictures. For a transparent PNG, use Finder: right-click the file and choose Quick Actions > Remove Background.

Part of the toolbar is hidden.

Make the window wider, or scroll the toolbar sideways with the trackpad.

Still need help?

Contact support

This opens the Quick Canvas support page on GitHub. Choose New issue and describe the problem, your macOS version, and the steps that lead to it. A screenshot helps. Please do not include personal information.
