Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Quick Canvas does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. You never sign in.
  • No tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party services.
  • No uploads. Quick Canvas sends nothing to any server.

Your pictures

Pictures you create or open stay on your Mac and are saved only where you choose. Saved files contain only your picture: no watermark, no author, no location, no camera details and no system information.

AI features

Create Image, Reimagine Picture and Remove Background use Apple's own on-device frameworks (Image Playground and Vision). Quick Canvas does not send your pictures anywhere. Apple's handling of Apple Intelligence is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Quick Canvas collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the Quick Canvas support page.
