The window

  • Title bar: Save, Undo and Redo buttons.
  • Toolbar: tools arranged in groups: Selection, Image, AI, Tools, Brushes, Shapes, Options, Colours. If the window is narrow, scroll the toolbar sideways.
  • Canvas: your picture. The pointer changes to match the tool you picked.
  • Status bar: pointer position, picture size, selection size, and zoom.

Colours

  • Colour 1 is used by the left mouse button. Colour 2 is used by the right mouse button, the eraser, and as the fill behind anything you move or delete.
  • Click Colour 1 or Colour 2 to make it active, then click a palette square to change it.
  • The rounded button beside the palette opens the full colour picker.
  • Opacity makes paint see-through. It applies to brushes, pencil, shapes, fill and text.

Drawing tools

ToolHow to use it

Shapes

  1. Click a shape, then drag on the canvas.
  2. Choose Outline, Filled or Outline + Fill in Options. With Outline + Fill, the outline uses Colour 1 and the inside uses Colour 2.
  3. Hold Shift while dragging for perfect squares and circles, or lines at 45 degree steps.
  4. Curve: drag a straight line first, then drag a second time anywhere to bend it.

Selecting and moving

  1. Pick the rectangle select or the free-form (lasso) select.
  2. Drag around the area.
  3. Drag inside the selection to move it, or use the arrow keys. Hold Shift with the arrow keys to move 10 pixels at a time.
  4. Press Return or Escape, or click outside, to drop it in place.
ActionShortcut
Copy, Cut, PasteCommand C, X, V

Crop the picture to the selectionCommand K

Pasting works with pictures copied from any other app.

Image menu

Resize Canvas (Shift Command R), Crop to Selection, Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate Right, Rotate Left, Clear Image. In Resize Canvas, tick "Stretch the picture" to scale the artwork; leave it off to add or remove space.

View

ActionShortcut
Zoom in, zoom out, actual sizeCommand =, Command -, Command 0
RulersCommand R
GridlinesCommand G

Gridlines are guides only. They are never saved into the picture.

Saving

  1. Choose File > Save As (Shift Command S).
  2. Pick a Format: PNG, JPG, JPEG, PDF, GIF, TIFF, BMP or HEIC.
  3. Pick a Quality: High quality keeps every detail and makes the largest file. Smaller file saves space. It makes JPG, JPEG, HEIC and TIFF files much smaller. PNG, GIF and BMP have no quality setting, so both choices give the same file.

Which format to choose:

You wantUse
Drawings, screenshots, sharp edgesPNG
Photos, or the smallest file to sendJPG
A document to print or attachPDF
Best quality at small size on Apple devicesHEIC

AI tools

ButtonWhat it does
Create ImageOpens Apple's Image Playground. Describe a picture; the result lands on the canvas as a selection you can move
Reimagine PictureSends your current picture to Image Playground as the starting point
Remove BackgroundKeeps the main subject and replaces everything else with Colour 2. Works best on photos with one clear person, animal or object

AI image creation needs a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15.2 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. Everything runs on your Mac.

Undo

Command Z undoes, Shift Command Z redoes. The last 40 steps are kept.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
The fill leaks out of a shapeThe outline has a gap. Zoom in, close the gap, and fill again
Arrow keys do nothingClick the canvas once so it has keyboard focus, then try again
The AI button says it is not availableCheck the requirements above; Intel Macs do not support Apple Intelligence
Part of the toolbar is hiddenWiden the window, or scroll the toolbar sideways
