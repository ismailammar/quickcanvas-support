How to convert HEIC to JPG on Mac

iPhones save photos as HEIC to save space, but some websites and Windows PCs prefer JPG. A Mac can convert them offline in seconds.

Method 1: Finder quick action

Right-click one or more HEIC files, choose Quick Actions > Convert Image, and pick JPEG or PNG.

Method 2: Preview

Open the photo, choose File > Export, set Format to JPEG, and save.

Method 3: Quick Canvas (convert and edit)

  1. Open the HEIC photo with File > Open.
  2. Crop, add text or remove the background if you like.
  3. Choose Save As, set Format to JPEG (.jpg), PNG or PDF, and pick a quality.
Quick Canvas saves files without camera or location data, so a converted photo does not reveal where it was taken.

Common questions

Does converting HEIC to JPG lose quality?

A little, as JPG is a compressed format. Choose High quality to keep the loss invisible, or PNG for no loss at a larger size.

Can I convert many HEIC files at once?

Yes, with the Finder quick action: select them all, right-click and choose Convert Image. Quick Canvas converts one picture at a time.
