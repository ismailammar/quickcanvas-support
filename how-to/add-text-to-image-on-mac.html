How to add text to a picture on Mac

Adding a caption, label or speech bubble to a picture takes under a minute on a Mac.

In Preview

Open the picture, show the Markup toolbar, click the Text button and type in the box that appears. Drag it into place.

In Quick Canvas

  1. Open the picture.
  2. Choose a text size in Options and a colour as Colour 1.
  3. Pick the Text tool (Aa), click where the text should start, type, and press Return.
  4. For a speech bubble, first draw a rounded rectangle with Outline + Fill (outline in Colour 1, inside in Colour 2), then type inside it.
  5. Lower Opacity before typing for see-through text, such as a light label over a photo.
  6. Save as JPG, PNG or PDF.

Common questions

Can I change the text after placing it?

In Quick Canvas, text becomes part of the picture once you press Return. Use Command Z to undo and type it again.

How do I make text stand out on a busy photo?

Draw a filled shape behind it first, or use a light colour on a dark area and the reverse.
