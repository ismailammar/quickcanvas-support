How to remove a background on Mac

macOS can separate a person, pet or object from its background on the device itself, with no upload and no subscription. Here are three ways, depending on what you need.

Method 1: Finder quick action (transparent PNG)

  1. Right-click the image file in Finder.
  2. Choose Quick Actions > Remove Background.
  3. A new copy with a transparent background appears next to the original.

Method 2: Preview

Open the photo in Preview and choose Tools > Remove Background.

Method 3: Quick Canvas (remove, then keep editing)

  1. Open the photo in Quick Canvas.
  2. Set Colour 2 to the background you want, for example white.
  3. Click the Remove background button in the AI group, or choose AI > Remove Background.
  4. The main subject stays and everything else becomes Colour 2. Then add text, shapes or crop, and save in any format.
Quick Canvas replaces the background with a solid colour rather than transparency. If you need a transparent PNG, use the Finder quick action first. Remove Background works best on photos with one clear person, animal or object, and needs macOS 14 or later.

Common questions

Can I remove a background on Mac without Photoshop?

Yes. macOS has on-device background removal in Finder quick actions and Preview, and Quick Canvas offers it as a one-click button.

Is the photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All three methods run on your Mac using Apple's own frameworks.

Why did it say no subject was found?

Background removal looks for a clear main subject. Drawings, landscapes and busy scenes often have none.
